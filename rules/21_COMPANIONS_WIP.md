## 1. Definition

**ESTABLISHED:** Every party member carries a past, a story, movable alignment, potential development in several directions, a preset for different possible powers, event-triggered reactions (possibly hard-coded), and relationships that can draw them closer to or further from the party or other members.

A companion is not a completed build but a **vessel of potential history** — different events and choices can produce different developments from the same starting person.

## 2. What Events May Do

- Awaken the past.
- Reveal hidden character.
- Move alignment.
- Strengthen or fracture loyalty.
- Unlock, weaken, transform, or destroy potential.
- Trigger refusal, departure, betrayal, sacrifice, or independent action.

## 3. Simultaneous Function

Companions simultaneously function as: parts of the company's power, sources of uncertainty, carriers of memory, participants in the company's emerging soul, and possible threads of destiny. The player constructs the company, but its human material is never completely controllable.

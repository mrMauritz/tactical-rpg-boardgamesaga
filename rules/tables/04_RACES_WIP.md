## 2. Human Cultures

Cultures of the game world — inspired by the saga world's directions, not by historical nations. Race or faction is OPEN (`22` §2 treats each race as a faction). Foreign gear and classes come from the non-Norse cultures.

| Culture | Explanation name | Gist | Inspiration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Norðmenn** | Northmen | The home culture | *Heimskringla*; *Íslendingabók* |
| **Gandfólk** ⚠️ coined | Sorcerer-folk of the far north | Spirit-workers, reindeer, enchanted hides | The saga Finnar — sorcerers of the north (*Egils saga*; *Óláfs saga helga*); *gandr*, magic |
| **Vestmenn** | Westmen | Isle-folk of the western sea; nature-priests | The saga word for the people of the western isles (*Landnámabók*) |
| **Suðrmenn** | Southerners | Southern kingdoms; knights, plate, oath-warriors | The saga word for southerners |
| **Austrríki** ⚠️ coined | The Eastern Realm | A great eastern empire; engineers, alchemists, guards | The saga's Great City of the east (*Laxdæla saga* 73) |

Trolls and giants (jötnar) are monsters (`05_MONSTERS_WIP.md`); spirits are divinities (`03_DIVINITIES_WIP.md` §3); Hamrammr is a class (`06_CLASSES_WIP.md`).

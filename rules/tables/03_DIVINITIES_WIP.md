## 3. Spirits

Wild casters reach the Wyrd through the spirits (`01_INTERNAL` Axis 2, LOCKED 2026-11-21).

| Spirit | Gist | Explanation | Root |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Landvættir** | Land-spirits | Guardians of a land; a níðstöng turns them against its ruler | *Egils saga* 57; *Óláfs saga Tryggvasonar* 33 (Heimskringla); *Landnámabók* |
| **Dísir** | Guardian women-spirits | Female guardian spirits honoured at the *dísablót* | *Ynglinga saga* 29; *Egils saga* 44 |
| **Fylgjur** | Follower-spirits | A spirit that follows a person, often seen as an animal or a woman | *Njáls saga* 41, 62; *Hallfreðar saga* 11 |
| **Hamingja** | Kin-luck | The luck of a kin, which can pass from one member to another | *Vatnsdæla saga* |
| **Valkyrjur** | Choosers of the slain | Decide who falls in battle and bring them to Odin | *Völuspá* 30; *Gylfaginning* 36 |
| **Nornir** | Fate-weavers | Shape the fate of all beings — ⚠️ whether they can be bonded at all is OPEN (Pillar of Mystery, `00A` §3) | *Völuspá* 20 |
| **Ancestors** | Blessed dead | The revered mound-dead — Óláfr Geirstaðaálfr worshipped in his mound. Overlap: the same dead may be a draugr (`05_MONSTERS_WIP.md`); the relationship decides | *Flateyjarbók* |

Overlap: the Álfar are a race (`04_RACES_WIP.md`), but also received worship (*álfablót*, *Kormáks saga*).

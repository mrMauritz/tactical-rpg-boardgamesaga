> **DEVELOPMENT STATUS: FOUNDATIONAL SPARK.** "Classes" is the working name. Names follow the naming rule: source-backed first, generic fantasy second, keeping the idea behind the earlier agent-made name.

> **WHEN TO READ:** Only on a specific request for class entries. The trees, perk restrictions, traditions, Craft and Mounted trees are owned by `../99_CLASS_TREES_WIP.md`; skills by `../03_skill_system.md`; bonds by `../24_BONDS_AND_DEVOTION_WIP.md`; devotion-requiring classes by `../catalogues/03_HISTORICAL_CLASSES_WIP.md`. ---

> A character is framed by saga-style description and **names earned in play** (`01c` §6) — this list is reference for how investment reads, not a set of categories a player picks.
>
> **A class connects to all three progressions** (`01c` §3): the **skills** it uses, the **trees** that give it its tactical direction, and the **bonds** that tie it to the world. Skills and bonds below are **suggestions**; the tree columns are the tactical side.

**Foreign** — from a culture outside the Norse world (`04_RACES_WIP.md` §2), with its own development curve.

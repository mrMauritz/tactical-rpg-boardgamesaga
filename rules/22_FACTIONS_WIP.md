## 1. Definition

**ESTABLISHED:** Factions work through the same vessel structure as Companions (`21_COMPANIONS_WIP.md`), at a larger, worldly scale. A faction is not a fixed label like "hostile kingdom" — it is a vessel capable of different historical movement. The player can influence a faction but does not completely control it.

| Companion scale | Faction scale |
| :--- | :--- |
| Personal past | Collective history |
| Character/temperament | Culture and institutional character |
| Movable alignment | Changing political and moral orientation |
| Skills and abilities | Military, economic, diplomatic, spiritual capacities |
| Personal relationships | Alliances, rivalries, dependencies, feuds |
| Memories, wounds, debts | Historical grievances, debts, obligations |
| Behavioural triggers | Doctrines, taboos, succession rules, crisis responses |
| Departure/betrayal | Secession, rebellion, or collapse |

> **Companions give the company a unique soul. Factions give the world a unique historical direction.**

## 2. Known Factions

**ESTABLISHED:** Each race is treated as a faction (see `21_COMPANIONS_WIP.md` on race as flavor-tendency, not attribute — a race-as-faction is a separate, worldly-political fact from a race's flavor identity). Within the Human Empire, three main factional powers:

1. **Trade**
2. **Nobility**
3. **Religion** — a worldly institution, kept distinct from Divinity bonds (`23_DIVINITIES_WIP.md`).

*Full faction roster beyond these three is OPEN — see `90_TODO_development_tracking.md` §5.*

## 3. Relationship to Bonds

Faction bonds are one of the Bond families (`24_BONDS_AND_DEVOTION_WIP.md` §2.1) — a bond's rewards, point tree, and consequences must manifest through that faction's actual presence, resources, institutions, members, and behavior. Do not treat faction rewards as abstract categories detached from the faction itself.

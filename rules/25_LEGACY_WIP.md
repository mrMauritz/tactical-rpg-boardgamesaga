## 1. Definition

**ESTABLISHED:** Legacy is the permanent mark left beyond individual life and beyond one playthrough.

> **A saga ends, but something created by it remains.**

Manifestations: character-creation unlocks; replayability unlocks; achievement unlocks; persistent records (names, titles, relics, bloodlines, memorials, chronicles); inherited possibilities made available by an earlier saga.

## 2. Persistent World Option

**ESTABLISHED:** Legacy also opens the option to save the world map and advance elapsed time into the next playthrough. A later playthrough may: use the same world/map; begin after time has passed; use a new main character and company; preserve changed settlements, borders, factions, and relationships (`22_FACTIONS_WIP.md`); retain earlier deaths, victories, ruins, foundations, relics, and reputations; encounter an earlier company as history, legend, or inherited consequence.

| Replay form | Structure |
| :--- | :--- |
| New world | New possibility-horizon |
| Continued world | New saga inside the history produced by earlier sagas |

## 3. Three Temporal Layers

1. **Power** — what the company can do now.
2. **Destiny** — what becomes visible through the present saga (`20_CAMPAIGN_CORE_WIP.md` §3).
3. **Legacy** — what remains possible because the saga existed.

> **Build power → exercise freedom → reveal destiny → leave a legacy → time passes → another saga enters the changed world.**

The map becomes a vessel carrying history across sagas.

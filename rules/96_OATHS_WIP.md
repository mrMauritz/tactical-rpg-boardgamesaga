# 96 Oaths & Karma (WIP)

## 1. The Core Definition of Oaths
Oaths are the only layer of the cosmos with *direction*. They are an act of will imposed simultaneously on the Life-Current (Önd) and the Wyrd. Oaths bind, create moral gravity, and carry weight beyond death.

The Wyrd operates ON the Life-Current—pressing fate-patterns into the living medium. Oaths shape BOTH simultaneously—they direct the flow of the Life-Current and create fixed points in the Wyrd that resist the Norns' weaving. A broken oath tears the fabric at both levels at once.

## 2. The Duality of the Thread and the Tapestry
The Wyrd (Fate) and Önd (Life-Current) exist in a state of contradiction: they are simultaneously **personal** and **collective**.

- **The Personal Drop:** Every being has their own localized life-current and their own specific thread of fate. 
- **The Collective Ocean:** Every personal thread is woven into the universal tapestry. Every drop of life-current belongs to the same vast, world-spanning ocean.

Because of this duality, Oaths and actions of deep moral gravity (Karma) do not happen in isolation. 

## 3. The Ripples of Action (Micro vs Macro)
When a character acts with extreme loyalty or profound treachery, the effect manifests on both levels simultaneously:

1. **The Micro (Personal):** The individual's soul/fate is altered. They are scarred by betrayal, anchored by loyalty, or tethered by revenge.
2. **The Macro (Collective):** The surrounding fabric of reality "feels" the action. A massive betrayal doesn't just curse the betrayer; it corrupts the ambient Wyrd of the physical location where it occurred, turning the hex "sour". It casts a shadow over their entire bloodline/warband.

### Generational Remnants
When a character falls, their presence is "seeded" into the world as a persistent marker. A character's Alignment and Wyrd influence the nature of the remnant (e.g., *Hallowed* vs. *Cursed*).

## 4. The Indifferent Wyrd & Alignment Frameworks
The Wyrd does **not** judge, measure, or care. It is an indifferent fabric. Alignment is simply the *framework* (the architecture) a character uses to interact with that fabric.

When an Oath is made, a thread is pulled taut. When an Oath is broken, that thread violently snaps. The Wyrd does not punish the breaker; the physics of the snap simply interact with the character's specific Alignment framework.

### ORDER (The Framework of Structure)
*Order relies on geometric flawlessness, rigid laws, and divine Oaths. They build in iron and stone.*
*   **The Oath:** A highly structured, load-bearing pillar in their soul.
*   **The Break (Betrayal):** **Structural Collapse**. Because their power (The Blessed Guard, The Silk-Sworn) requires perfect tension, snapping a thread causes their internal architecture to shatter. Their "Wyrd-Scar" is a loss of foundation and passive protections. The world isn't punishing them; their engine simply requires perfect geometry to run, and they just broke a gear.

### WILD (The Framework of Symbiosis)
*Wild relies on adaptation, feral rhythms, and deep roots in the Life-Current. They build in flesh, blood, and earth.*
*   **The Oath:** A living, breathing root system connecting them to the pack or the environment.
*   **The Break (Betrayal):** **Organic Infection**. When they break a bond, they aren't shattering stone—they are tearing out a living root. Their "Wyrd-Scar" is parasitic. The local Life-Current (the hexes, the animals, the ambient weather) naturally rejects them, not out of moral judgment, but the way a body rejects an infection.

### CHAOS (The Framework of Dissolution)
*Chaos is indifference. Survival of the fittest. The dissolvement of structure. They build in void and static.*
*   **The Oath:** Chaos characters do not build pillars or roots. If they forge a bond, it is a volatile, predatory tether—a temporary exploit to ensure survival.
*   **The Break (Betrayal):** **Volatile Release**. There is no rigid pillar to shatter, and no organic root to bleed. Because Chaos thrives on dissolving structure, snapping the thread causes immense collateral damage to *others* (the winner takes all). However, their "Wyrd-Scar" is the acceleration of their own un-making. The more tethers they dissolve to survive, the more isolated they become, eventually risking total "Hollowing" (complete severance from the Life-Current).

## 5. The Origins of Direction (The Tension of the Bowstring)
The Life-Current (Önd) naturally just wants to flow — chaotic, spreading like water. An Oath is the act of forcing that water into a channel. Oaths are born from three distinct fires:

*   **Necessity (The Vow of Survival):** Sworn when backed against a cliff, exchanging future sovereignty for immediate survival. Transactional and desperate. Often the most fragile, as the need that created it may pass while the Wyrd-thread remains.
*   **Custom (The Social Bond):** The everyday weave of civilization. The arm-ring. The laws of hospitality. The sworn allegiance to a Jarl. Inherited structure — not always felt deeply, but the Wyrd does not distinguish between a heartfelt vow and a customary one. The thread is the thread.
*   **[NAME PENDING] (The Conviction-Oath):** Sworn not out of fear or tradition, but from a terrifying internal clarity. *I see what must be done, and I will make reality conform to it.* The most dangerous kind, because it requires no external validation. A Chaos character can swear this oath without contradiction — it imposes their own structure rather than submitting to another's.

### The Tension
Because an Oath forces *direction* upon the natural flow of the world, it creates **Tension**. An Oath is not a static object — it is a stretched bowstring in the Wyrd. The stronger the Oath, the tighter the string. An Oath-bound character carries this tension in their body and soul at all times. They are capable of immense power when that tension releases in the right direction. But they are always fighting the natural "flow" of reality around them, which simply wants to spread and settle.

## 6. The Sacrifice (The Cost of Maintenance)
A deep Wyrd-bond is not free. The Law of Mutual Sacrifice runs through everything in this world — and Oaths are no exception. Maintaining the tension of a stretched bowstring requires constant, silent sacrifice.

*   **The Metabolic Cost (The Heavy Soul):** Holding a deep oath burns the Life-Current continuously. The character's Önd is never allowed to fully settle or rest. They may require more sustenance, sleep poorly, or carry a physical heaviness that others sense. They are a pressurized vessel, always.
*   **The Spiritual Cost (The Given Thread):** When you swear an Oath to a King, you are literally giving him a fragment of your Óðr. You are no longer entirely your own master in the eyes of the Norns. If the King is cursed, that shadow touches you, because he holds your thread. To be deeply Oath-bound is to be powerful — and to be a partial slave to the structure you built. This is the ultimate tension: the sacrifice of sovereignty for the power of direction.

## 7. The Oath-Web (The Traveling Scar)
Oaths are not isolated lines between two people. They are a vast, interconnected web holding civilization together. This is where the system becomes truly beautiful and dangerous.

*   **The Chain:** A Jarl gives an arm-ring to a Huskarl, tying their Wyrd-threads together. The Huskarl then swears to protect a merchant. The Jarl's thread is now indirectly connected to the merchant's fate.
*   **The Traveling Rot:** If the Huskarl breaks the oath and murders the merchant, the thread violently snaps. The Huskarl gains a deep Wyrd-Scar. But because the Jarl holds the other end of the Huskarl's thread, the violent vibration — the "Rot" — travels up the chain. The Jarl begins to feel Spiritual Friction. Their own Wyrd, their luck, their hallowed protections, begin to sour — because they are anchored to a rotting thread.
*   **The Cosmological Mandate:** The Jarl is not punished by the Gods or by society. They are compelled by the physics of the Wyrd itself. To stop the rot, they must act. They must sever the bond — by outlawing, executing, or formally cursing the Huskarl — or the rot will continue to travel upward until it reaches the Jarl's own core thread. Kings do not punish traitors merely out of human law. They punish them because if they do not, the traitor's Wyrd-Scar will infect the kingdom itself.

## 8. The Nature of Bonds (Knots & Tethers)
Because Alignment dictates the framework, positive and aggressive bonds manifest differently.

### Saga-Knots (Steadfastness / Coming to Aid)
*When characters anchor themselves to each other in extreme loyalty.*
*   **Order (The Shield-Wall):** An unbreakable, perfect geometric weave. (Mechanically: Hard damage reduction or shared defense values).
*   **Wild (The Pack-Bond):** A deep symbiotic root. (Mechanically: Shared HP pools, or gaining feral bonuses when fighting adjacent to the bonded ally).
*   **Chaos (The Sovereign Pact):** An alliance of convenience or sheer dominant will. (Mechanically: A volatile bond where one character might absorb the other's pain to ensure the "fittest" survives).

### The Blood-Tether (Revenge)
*The violent, physical carving of a vendetta.*
*   **Order (Retribution):** A rigid, unyielding duel. The tether forces the avenger to fight with perfect, terrifying focus, ignoring all distractions until justice is met.
*   **Wild (The Hunt):** A primal tracking instinct. The tether allows the avenger to scent the target's Önd across the map, gaining immense speed and ferocity.
*   **Chaos (Annihilation):** Absolute indifference to collateral damage. The tether allows the avenger to dissolve reality (ignoring cover/armor) to reach the target, pulling the whole hex down with them if necessary.

    *   *The Law Branch:* Negotiation and oath-brokering.

## 10. Oath-Bound Filters (The Blessed Guard)
Certain characters (like Temple Guardians) are structurally Oath-Bound. Their physical frame is forged cleanly into the Wyrd, granting passive resistance against unnatural/cursed manipulations. However, acting against their Patron's nature triggers the Structural Collapse described above, severing their connection to the Wyrd entirely until atonement is made.

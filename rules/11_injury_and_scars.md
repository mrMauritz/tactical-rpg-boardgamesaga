## 1. Post-Battle Resolution (LOCKED 2026-11-18)

There is a **post-battle resolution**. It is one of the times scars attach to a character — not the only one.

> *Surviving the battlefield was only the first check; surviving the aftermath was the second* (`95_HISTORICAL_COMBAT_WIP.md` §9).

**In the sources:** after Stiklastaðir a woman tends the wounded and tests a gut wound with onion broth; Þormóðr pulls the arrow from his own chest (*Óláfs saga helga* ch. 234, Heimskringla; *Fóstbræðra saga* ch. 24). The law grades wounds: the "major wounds" (*meiri sár*) — to the brain, into the body cavity, to the marrow — are a legal category (*Grágás*, Vígslóði).

### 1.2 One Wound, Several Things

In the sources a wound is several things at once. **The event decides** which apply (`27_EVENTS.md` §1.1, integration principle):

- **A scar** on the body — this chapter.
- **Grounds for compensation** — wound severity sets what can be claimed (*Grágás*, Vígslóði; `27_EVENTS.md` §4; `20b_STRATEGY_AND_BALANCE_MAP_WIP.md` §7.6 Feud Resolution).
- **An earned name** — Önundr *tréfótr*, for the leg lost at Hafrsfjörðr (*Grettis saga* ch. 2; `01c_CHARACTER_BUILD_PIPELINE_WIP.md` §6).
- **Part of fame** — *orðstírr* (`27_EVENTS.md` §6).

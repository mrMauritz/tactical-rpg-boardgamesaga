## 1. Origin: What the Campaign Layer Learns from RogueTech & KARDS

**ESTABLISHED parallels:** companions ~ mechs/cards; skills ~ familiarity; open world ~ open galaxy; the party may be mercenary but may become much more; magic/spiritual reality can occupy an analogous invisible strategic layer without copying Electronic Warfare.

RogueTech's strength is a clear central activity — **the player constructs a war machine** — through a strong feedback loop: **Acquisition → construction → experimentation → consequence**. KARDS reinforces the same pattern through deck construction: concrete construction, many combinations, continual experimentation, immediate feedback, no permanently final build.

## 2. The Deeper Construction

BG Saga's deeper structure is not "build a war machine" but:

> **The player constructs the power to determine the company's own course.**

Military force is one form of power. Others: the power to protect, persuade, command, negotiate, know, travel, endure, escape, avoid, remain independent, heal, and act within the spiritual world. Freedom is materially produced by the party's actual capabilities — companions, equipment, skills, knowledge, relationships, wealth, reputation, factional access, bonds, spiritual power — not a menu of options.

Core transformation: **Gain capabilities → expand real freedom → choose a path → accept consequences → transform the company.**

## 3. Freedom, Uncertainty, and Destiny

**ESTABLISHED:** Destiny must gradually become visible but must never become a predictable route assembled by the player — that would replace saga with planning.

- The player builds power in order to choose.
- The world remains uncertain and cannot be fully read.
- Choices have consequences beyond intention.
- The company gradually acquires a unique character/soul.
- Destiny becomes visible only through what happens.

> **Power gives the company freedom to act. The unknown gives its actions meaning. Destiny is revealed where the two meet.**

## 4. Replayability

Replayability should arise from repeatedly constructing different forms of power and discovering what saga each construction makes possible — not mainly from alternate scripted content. Recurring qualities to preserve: concrete construction, multiple viable combinations, continued acquisition, testing through conflict/events, clear evidence of whether a construction works, no permanently completed form, familiar rules generating unfamiliar situations.

Two open development fields: **(1)** challenges/possibilities for different forms of power, **(2)** different possible destinies in the world. Challenges test power; opportunities expand power; destiny emerges through power's acquisition and use but is not reducible to a build outcome.

## 5. Giving the Structure Color, Smell, and Shape

- Power becomes tangible through people, weapons, horses, ships, wealth, knowledge, alliances, reputation, magic, refuge.
- Freedom becomes visible through opportunities the party can pursue, refuse, escape, negotiate, or transform.
- The unknown becomes present through rumours, concealed motives, unfamiliar places, incomplete knowledge, delayed consequences.
- Destiny leaves traces through scars, enemies, loyalties, names, losses, possessions, changed settlements, stories told.
- The company's soul grows through repeated actions and shared ordeals — not through selecting an identity.

Atmospheric clues on record: mud, smoke, winter roads, crowded halls, damaged weapons, frightened horses, old obligations, unfamiliar customs, sacred places, rumours of uncertain truth.

> **You gather people and power around you. You enter a world you cannot fully read. Your choices leave marks upon both the company and the world. Only gradually do you understand what kind of saga you are living.**

## 7. Compact System Map

| Scale | Vessel | Domain | Development |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Individual | Companion | Personal world | Character, capability, personal destiny |
| Company | Party/main character | Lived saga | Collective power, freedom, cohesion, soul |
| Historical | Faction | Actual/worldly sphere | Political power, historical direction |
| Spiritual | Divinity | Divine/spiritual sphere | Meaning, sacred possibility, mythic movement |
| Trans-saga | Legacy/map | Persisting world | History and possibilities inherited by later playthroughs |

**Core causal structure:** the world presents uncertain possibilities → possibilities exert pull (`26_CAMPAIGN_ECONOMY_WIP.md`) → the player selects companions, missions, bonds, investments → these produce concrete forms of power → power expands actual freedom → requirements/taboos/costs/exclusivities shape what remains possible → party bonds and shared history contribute to cohesion → tolerance contains differences among companions → the party acts without complete knowledge → consequences transform characters, factions, divinities, places, future options → a unique destiny becomes visible retrospectively and gradually → the saga leaves legacy within unlocks or a persistent world (`25_LEGACY_WIP.md`).

## 8. Guardrails (Campaign-Layer Laws)

1. Do not make survival pressure the main motivational motor — pull, not push, gives movement direction (`26_CAMPAIGN_ECONOMY_WIP.md`).
2. Do not turn destiny into a route selected in advance.
3. Do not make bonds generic bonus packages detached from actual powers — faction effects must come from the actual faction; divine effects from the actual spiritual bond.
4. Do not make divinity a generic magic-school label — it is a concrete spiritual relation.
5. Do not treat dedication as necessarily worship — it may be study or disciplined practice.
6. Do not impose universal bond slots — restrictions are specific to the bond and bearer.
7. Do not make progression freely reversible.
8. Do not make every bond purely beneficial — most bond-tree benefits carry costs.
9. Do not punish one character for another's acts without an explicit event rule.
10. Do not add separate systems when complexity can emerge from existing interactions (§6).
11. Preserve uncertainty — the player may understand the rules without fully understanding the world or destiny.

## 9. Central Design Kernel

> **The player gathers people and constructs power in order to become free. Bonds to factions, divinities and dedications create pull, open concrete possibilities and shape the party's future through benefits, costs, requirements and taboos. Yet companions, factions, spiritual powers and the world possess histories and movements of their own. The company can choose its actions but cannot know their complete meaning or consequences. Through the meeting of acquired freedom and an uncertain world, the soul and destiny of each unique saga gradually become visible. When that saga ends, its legacy may persist in future characters, unlocks or the continued history of the same world.**

> **Build power. Gain freedom. Follow the pull. Meet the unknown. Reveal a destiny. Leave a legacy.**
